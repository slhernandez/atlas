What is the flywheel?

How Atlas gets better at your codebase. Frictions go into the scorecard's friction log. Friction-log entries become edits: to HOUSE_RULES.md, a template, or an agent. And the journal feeds every future run the research, plans and decisions of the runs before it.

It arrives good. It becomes yours.

The factory that built your last feature is not the one that builds your next. The redundancy check lets it shrink as well as grow.

Supervising AI on a real codebase?

Atlas is a Claude Code plugin: three roles, two hard gates, and a journal that remembers. MIT licensed.
